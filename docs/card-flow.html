Problem zone Content is fully visible (opacity 1) when the FLIP starts with scale(~2.0). Children inherit the parent's transform — content appears at 2× its natural size on the first frame, then scales down visually as the FLIP resolves. On open this is hidden by opacity: the content is invisible during the worst distortion. On close, it isn't.
