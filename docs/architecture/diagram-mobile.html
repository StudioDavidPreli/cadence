Context: When the .library container is ≤600px (sidebar collapsed, narrow viewport, or single-panel mobile view), the @container rule in PrincipleCard.module.css fires and flips expandedContent to flex-direction: column.
Grid at ~320px container: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(180px, 1fr)) → 1 column, 272px wide (320 - 2×24 padding). Row height fixed at 234px.
2×2 footprint with 1 column: gridColumn: 1 / span 2 is clamped to the single column — card stays 272px wide. gridRow: 1 / span 2 gives 2 rows = 480px tall. Card is tall and narrow: 272×480px.
Known issue (β): stacked animationHalf (min-height:240px) + contentHalf (~120px) + quoteBlock (~158px) = ~518px, which exceeds 480px. Deferred fix: overflow-y:auto on expandedWrapper in the @container rule.
